Comments should read like a human wrote them — no fancy grids, no padding, no extra whitespace, no decorative structure. Reference style: https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/broad/commkit/coding-and-comment-style/

**Why:** Overformatted comments add noise without value. The goal is readability, not decoration.

**How to apply:** Write comments tight and plain. No box-drawing, no aligned columns of whitespace, no banner headers. Just a short, clear line when the "why" isn't obvious.
